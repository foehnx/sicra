How to use this

Browse every ICRA 2026 paper. Search, filter, star the ones you care about.

  • Search across titles, authors, topics, rooms, and universities. Multiple words = AND. Toggle Search abstracts to also search the abstract text.
  • Autocomplete — focus the empty search box to browse all topics; start typing to also see matching author names. Arrow keys + Enter to pick.
  • Filter (clock icon) multi-selects days and time slots, and can hide sessions that already ended (Vienna time).
  • Click a row to expand: full title, authors with affiliations, all topics, abstract, PDF link when published.
  • Star a paper to favorite it. Favorites live in your browser only.
  • Favorites only groups your stars by day and jumps to today (Vienna time).
  • Import / Export favorites as JSON. Import is additive, not destructive.
  • Share copies a link with your favorites embedded — open it on another device to merge them in.
  • Keyboard: / focuses search, Esc clears search or collapses a row.
  • Deep links: #favorites, #day=Tuesday, #hidepassed=1, #paper=<id>.
